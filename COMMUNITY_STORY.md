# Yellow Zone — The Missing Dataset

The Yellow Zone investigation is one continuous data-recovery RPG, not five independent CTFs. The player receives a corrupted maintenance broadcast that references a dataset omitted from Sanctuary Zero’s public registry. MANU reports perfect continuity; surviving records show that the public view and the underlying ledger no longer agree.

## Player-facing chapter flow

1. **Hospital — The record that should not exist.** An active intake was rewritten as EMPTY. A paper copy and printer queue survived. Their reconciliation footer points toward civic export records.
2. **School — The roster with eighteen empty lines.** A teacher’s earlier roster and later tablet export disagree. The archive preserves a version comparison and an old export cover sheet. A supporting catalog reference leads to the Museum.
3. **Museum — The image with its edge removed.** An original gallery image and conservation manifest disagree with the public label. A preservation receipt shows that the image passed through a timing relay.
4. **Sports Complex — The replay that starts too late.** The public scoreboard replay begins after the raw timing buffer. A transfer acknowledgement survives, but its destination is masked. The address format points to a residential service node.
5. **Society — The address removed from the map.** The public housing map omits a physical unit while resident records disappeared from the public board but were retained in a reconciliation ledger. The five traces can now be reconciled into one recovery signature.

## Interface requirements

- Every building terminal should show the chapter number, an incident report, why the node matters, its incoming trace, the narrative handoff to the next node, and the current chapter objective.
- The trace-network panel and map show the same numbered route and a short description of each chapter.
- One designated fictional social account is accessible per building. Cross-account references may appear inside comments as evidence, but players cannot switch profiles from the local terminal.
- Posts, highlights, stories, notes, timestamps, comments, and visual evidence should support the same investigation. No isolated building flags or unrelated minigames.
- The shared final flag is submitted from the main Yellow Zone map in the **FINAL FLAG SUBMISSION** panel, not inside a building terminal. A correct signature reveals the recovered Continuity Ledger on the main map.
